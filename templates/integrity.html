🛡️ Three Levels of Integrity Protection

  • AES-GCM Authentication Tag — 128-bit tag automatically verified on every decryption. Any ciphertext modification causes immediate failure.
  • SHA-256 Digest — Hash of ciphertext stored in DB. Detects accidental corruption. NOT tamper-proof alone (attacker could modify both object and hash).
  • HMAC-SHA-256 Reference — Keyed MAC over (record_id + ciphertext). Cannot be forged without the integrity key. Provides strong tamper-evidence.
